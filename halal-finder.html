How a restaurant earns the badge:

Three checks, done in person and recorded. A restaurant that fails any one of them is never shown as verified.

Certificate:

We ask to see the original halal certificate, and record who issued it and when it expires. A photo goes on the listing, so you can read it yourself.

Supplier:

We trace the meat back to its supplier and check the supplier’s own papers, not just the restaurant’s word.

Kitchen:

We visit in person, photograph what we are permitted to, and date-stamp the check. Badges lapse, and every place is inspected again.
